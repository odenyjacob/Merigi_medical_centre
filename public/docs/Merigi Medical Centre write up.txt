Merigi Medical Centre:

1. Primary Navigation (The Header)
This is what users see on every page. Keep it clean and functional.

Home: A high-level overview of the centre.

About Us: Mission, vision, and the history of the clinic.

Services: A dropdown menu of specialties (e.g., General Consultation, Laboratory, Pharmacy, Maternity).

Our Team: Profiles of doctors and specialists to build patient rapport.

Patient Portal/Information: Insurance partners, billing info, and "What to expect."

Contact Us: Map, phone numbers, and emergency contact.

2. Essential Page Breakdowns
Home Page (The "Front Desk")
Hero Section: A high-quality photo of the facility with a clear "Book Appointment" button.

Quick Links: Icons for "Find a Doctor," "Emergency Services," and "View Results."

Operating Hours: Clearly visible (especially if you have 24/7 services).

Testimonials: Brief quotes from satisfied patients.

Services Page (The "Clinical Core")
Instead of one long list, categorize your offerings:

Outpatient Care: General check-ups, immunizations.

Inpatient Care: Ward information, nursing care.

Diagnostics: X-ray, Ultrasound, Lab tests.

Specialized Clinics: Antenatal, Diabetes management, Pediatrics.

Contact & Location
Google Maps Integration: So patients can get GPS directions instantly.

Direct Call Button: Vital for mobile users in an emergency.

Inquiry Form: For non-urgent questions.

3. The Footer (The "Safety Net")
Emergency Contact: Highlighted in red or bold.

Social Media Links: Facebook or LinkedIn for community updates.

Legal: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Newsletter Signup: For health tips and facility updates.
