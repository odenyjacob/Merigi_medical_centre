Our Services
At Merigi Medical Centre, we provide compassionate, accessible, and high-quality healthcare that brings healing and hope to our community. Our skilled team ensures patient-centered care across a range of services:
1. Outpatient Services
Our outpatient services offer prompt medical consultation, diagnosis, and treatment for all age groups. We ensure timely care so you can get back to your daily life quickly, with confidence in our expertise.
2. Inpatient Services
For patients needing close monitoring or specialized care, our inpatient services provide comfortable, safe, and well-equipped rooms. Our team offers continuous attention to support your recovery.
3. Maternity Services
We provide comprehensive maternity care, including antenatal, delivery, and postnatal support. Our focus is on safe and compassionate care for both mother and baby, available 24/7.
4. Laboratory Services
Our laboratory offers accurate and timely diagnostic tests to support effective treatment. We use modern equipment and follow strict quality standards for reliable results.
5. Pharmacy Services
We provide a wide range of quality, affordable medicines with professional guidance to ensure the right treatment for every patient.
6. Nutrition Services
Our nutrition team offers dietary advice and wellness support to help you maintain a healthy lifestyle and manage chronic conditions.
7. Circumcision Services
We offer safe, hygienic, and medically supervised circumcision procedures for all ages, performed by trained healthcare professionals.
8. Ultrasound Services
Our ultrasound services provide accurate imaging for pregnancy monitoring, abdominal scans, and other diagnostic needs, helping our clinicians make informed decisions.
9. X-Ray Services
We provide modern X-ray imaging to support fast and accurate diagnosis of fractures, chest conditions, and other medical concerns.
